How to play

Build: pick a tower in the Kitchen (the key is shown on each card), then click open ground. Hold Shift to keep placing.
Upgrade: click a tower, then Upgrade (U). Sell refunds 70% (Delete, click twice).
Waves: Space sends the next wave, even while one is still walking. Early waves pay a bonus.
Speed: F cycles 1×, 2×, 3× and 4×. P or Esc opens the pause menu, where you can restart the map. M skips the song. H switches music between HARDCORE, MEDIUM and CHILL MIX. Extra instruments join in depending on which critters are on screen.
Powers: every tower has one. Upgrade a tower to tier 6 and its power appears above the map. Fire it by clicking or Shift + the tower's key. Powers only recharge while critters are on the trail.
Wave bar: under the map. Click the highlighted wave to send it. With auto-send on, the red timer sends it for you.
Elites: every 3rd wave. Watch their tricks: stink clouds knock out towers, white tigers freeze them, flaming tigers are fireproof.
Targeting: aimed towers can focus First, Last, Strong or Close critters. Snipers default to Strong.
Armor: big critters shrug off small hits. Baguettes, fizz and fire get through armor better than popcorn.
Diamonds: press D for the Diamond Den. Each tower type has 5 star ranks, every star adds a perk to all towers of that type.
Tower paths: at tier 4 every tower picks path A, B or C. B and C are unlocked forever in Grandma's Cookbook with Golden Spatulas.
Golden Spatulas: earned every wave and kept between runs. Spend them on permanent kitchen upgrades. Achievements boost how many you earn.
Hiding critters: anything inside a hollow log, underground or cloaked can't be targeted. Mines and poison still work.
