Painted Keep

Pip has a hammer, a hat, and a castle full of paintings. Jump into each painting, explore its world, and bring home the stars hidden inside.

Moves

W A S DRun. Mouse moves the camera, wheel zooms, arrow keys also turn it. SpaceJump. Jump again right as you land for a double, then a triple jump. ShiftCrouch. In the air: ground pound. Shift + SpaceStanding: backflip. Running: long jump. Reverse + SpaceTurn sharply while running, then jump: side flip. Space at a wallJump into a wall and press Space again to wall kick. Left clickHammer swing. In the air: hammer spin. Right clickDive. Belly slide when you land. EscPause. M toggles music.

Caps

FeatherJump, then press Space in the air to fly. W dives, S climbs, A/D steer, Shift lets go. IronHeavy and unbreakable. Walk on lava and flatten anything you touch. PhantomDrift through violet grates and ignore enemy hits.
