    How the fight works
    • Three messengers shield the boss: all three up means invulnerable, two means armored (DEF ×1.5), one means normal DEF 60, none means armor broken. Shoot them to knock them out; they come back after 45 s.
    • He chains 2–3 attacks, then 4–5 once exalted, and resets to the center in between.
    • Deal 3.5% of his HP during an attack to trigger the branch: guardable attacks raise a shield (stop shooting or get countered with 30 s of Weak, Sick, Quiet and Armor Broken); staggerable attacks stun him, then he guards.
    • Thresholds: portals 85%, dance 70%, exalted 55%, celestial 40%, heaven walls 15%, chest 1%. Celestial caps damage at 20% of his HP.
    • After celestial he teleports to you at the start of every attack and lightning strikes around you.
